# Code of conduct

This applies everywhere the project is discussed: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and
anywhere else you are speaking as part of it.

## What is expected

- **Treat people with respect.** Criticise code, designs and decisions, never the person who wrote
  them. A review that says what is wrong and why is welcome; one that sneers is not.
- **Assume good faith.** The interface ships in thirteen languages and a lot of the people here are
  writing in their second or third one. A blunt sentence is usually just a short one.
- **Be patient with questions.** Everyone was new to the YM2612 once.
- **Respect other people's work.** Credit where you found something, and only contribute what you
  have the right to contribute. [`docs/licences/ASSETS.md`](docs/licences/ASSETS.md) says which
  licences the project can take.
- **Accept a decision once it is made.** Disagreeing is fine, and so is making the case again when
  something new turns up. Reopening the same argument over and over is not. What the application
  deliberately does not do is written down in [`docs/FEATURES.md`](docs/FEATURES.md).

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, or insults, in public or in private.
- Slurs, and jokes or remarks aimed at anyone's identity, background or ability.
- Sexual content or attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Pushing someone for private contact after they have said no.
- Deliberately derailing threads, spamming, or flooding the project with low-effort submissions.

## Reporting a problem

If something here makes you uncomfortable, or you see it happen to someone else, report it
privately through the repository's
[private report form](https://github.com/MeguminBOT/md-synth-daw/security/advisories/new) and start
the title with **Conduct:**. Only the maintainers can see it. Say what happened, where, and link to
it if it is public.

Reports are kept confidential. The person you report is not told who reported them.

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers act in proportion to what happened. That can be a quiet word, an edit to or removal of
a comment, a locked thread, a warning, or a temporary or permanent block from the project. Someone
who is asked to stop and stops is usually the end of it.

Maintainers are held to this document the same as anyone else, and a report about a maintainer is
handled by the others.
